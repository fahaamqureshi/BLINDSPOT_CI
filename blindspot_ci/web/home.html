How a check works

  1. Pick photos a person labeledA person drew a box around the object in each photo. A model never writes the labels.
  2. The model outlines the objectThe model under test looks at each photo and draws its own box.
  3. Plain code scores itPython compares the two boxes. A test passes when they overlap enough. No model grades itself.
    What is IoU?

    IoU means intersection over union. It is the area the two boxes share divided by the area they cover together. 1.00 is a perfect match and 0.00 is no overlap.

What this is not

Not a safety approval. A person always decides. The check covers the listed model, photos and dates only. A pass means the model met the bar on those photos.

Try the overlap score

The score for each test is the overlap of two boxes. Move the amber box over the teal one and watch the number change. A test passes when the overlap is at least 0.35, which is the scorer's default.
